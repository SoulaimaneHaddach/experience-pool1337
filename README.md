# My 1337 Pool Experience

My experience during the 1337 Pool in July 2026 was honestly one of the best experiences I have ever had.

The vibe was amazing, the people were amazing, and the amount of motivation and energy you get when you're surrounded by people working hard is something I had never experienced before.

During those 26 days, I felt a lot of things at the same time: happiness, stress, excitement, frustration, motivation, sadness, and a lot of laughs. Sometimes it was crazy, and sometimes it was difficult.

One of the best parts was the people I met there. We met for the first time during the Pool, but after a few days, it felt like we had known each other for years. We worked together, helped each other, laughed together, struggled with the projects, and somehow survived those 26 days together.

I honestly don't know if I'm going to become a student at 1337, but I really hope so, hhh.

During the Pool, I gave an effort that I don't think I had ever given before. At some point, I told myself: "I want to be here. This is my place." So I tried to push myself more, forget the laziness, go beyond what I thought I could do, and make the most of those 26 days.

I don't really know how to fully explain this experience. It was much more than just programming or solving projects. Nothing I have experienced before feels quite the same. I did my best, made good friends, learned a lot, and pushed myself further than I expected.

No matter what happens next, those 26 days were an amazing experience for me. I learned a lot, met great people, and discovered how much effort I can actually give when I really want something.
